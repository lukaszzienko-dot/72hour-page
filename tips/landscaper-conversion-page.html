Landscaper conversion page that books yard work

A landscaper website often looks like a photo album. A conversion page is different: one page that tells homeowners what you do, which towns you cover, and how to request a quote — without making them hunt.

Name the work in the headline

Lead with the job and the place: “Lawn care and hardscape in Wayne and North Jersey,” not “Quality outdoor solutions.” Search and referral traffic decide in seconds whether you mow, mulch, install patios, or only commercial sites.

Show recent outdoor proof

Use before/after lawns, patio installs, or seasonal cleanup shots you already have. Add a short note on insurance or years in the area. Skip fake star testimonials — specific job photos convert better for local landscaping.

List towns next to services

Homeowners bounce when they cannot tell if you drive to their street. Put service towns near the top and pair them with a plain list: weekly maintenance, spring/fall cleanup, mulch, hardscape, snow if you offer it.

One primary CTA

Call or email as the main action, repeated in the hero, mid-page, and footer. A landscaper conversion page loses bookings when every social icon competes with the phone number.

Launch friction check

  • Tap-to-call phone works on mobile.
  • Email opens with a subject so the lead is ready to send.
  • Towns are readable above the fold on a phone screen.
  • No popup wall on first visit.
  • Title tags match the offer for search snippets.
