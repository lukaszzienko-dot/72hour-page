## How to use (15–90 minutes)

1. Open `page-template.html` in a text editor or browser.
2. Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` with your business details (see list below).
3. Run 2–3 prompts from `copy-prompts.md` to generate your headline, lede, and offer.
4. Follow `launch-checklist.md` to publish (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, your host, etc.).
5. Test the form and the phone link on your phone.

### Placeholders to replace

- `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}` — your company name  
- `{{TRADE}}` — e.g. HVAC, landscaping, roofing  
- `{{TOWNS}}` — towns you cover  
- `{{PHONE}}` — your booking number (digits + display)  
- `{{EMAIL}}` — where lead emails should open  
- `{{OFFER}}` — the one clear ask (free estimate, same-week visit, etc.)  
- `{{CITY_STATE}}` — e.g. Wayne, NJ  

**Do not put** street address, DOB, SSN, or other sensitive data on the page.
