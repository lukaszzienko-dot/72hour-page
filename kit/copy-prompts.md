# AI Copy Prompt Pack — Local Conversion Page

Paste these into ChatGPT, Claude, or similar. Fill the brackets. Keep outputs short and specific — local homeowners skim.

**Rules for every prompt:** No fake testimonials. No street address. No DOB/SSN. Phone + email only for contact. Wayne NJ–style local voice: plain, direct, job-focused.

---

## 1. Hero headline + lede

```
Write a landing-page hero for a [TRADE] business serving [TOWNS / REGION].
Audience: homeowners who need [JOB TYPE] soon, not browsers.
Tone: direct, trustworthy, no hype words like "revolutionary" or "disrupt".
Give me:
1) One headline under 10 words that promises the outcome (booked job / fast response).
2) One lede of 2 sentences max naming the trade and area.
3) One primary CTA button label (call or request estimate).
Do not invent awards, years in business, or review counts I did not give you.
Facts I approve: [PASTE ONLY TRUE FACTS].
```

---

## 2. Offer block (what they get)

```
Turn this service into one clear offer for a conversion page:
Service: [WHAT YOU DO]
Area: [TOWNS]
Promise: [e.g. same-week estimate / emergency call-back]
Price cue if any: [e.g. free estimate / starting at $X — or "call for quote"]
Output 3 short bullets: outcome, how fast, what happens after they contact us.
Max 18 words per bullet.
```

---

## 3. “What’s broken” empathy section

```
Write 3 short cards for homeowners who found us because something is wrong:
Trade: [TRADE]
Common problems: [LIST 3–5 REAL PROBLEMS]
Each card: bold 4–6 word title + one sentence. No fear-mongering. No medical/legal claims.
```

---

## 4. How it works (3 steps)

```
Write a 3-step "How it works" for booking [TRADE] work in [AREA].
Step 1 must start with them contacting us (call or form).
Step 2 is us confirming / scheduling.
Step 3 is the visit or job start.
Keep each step to a title + one sentence. No jargon.
```

---

## 5. FAQ (objections)

```
Write 5 FAQs for a [TRADE] landing page in [AREA].
Cover: response time, service area, estimate cost, insurance/licensing if I provide facts, and what to prepare for the visit.
Only use these facts: [PASTE].
If a fact is missing, write "Ask us when you call" instead of inventing it.
```

---

## 6. Form microcopy

```
Write form labels and a 1-line privacy note for a lead form that emails the contractor.
Fields: name, town, phone, short description of the job.
CTA button: [e.g. Send my request]
Privacy note: we only use this to call or email about this job. No DOB, SSN, or street address collected on the form.
```

---

## 7. SMS / missed-call follow-up (optional)

```
Write 3 short text-message templates (under 160 chars) a [TRADE] owner can send after a web lead:
1) Confirm receipt + ask best callback window
2) Offer two time slots
3) Soft close if no reply in 24h
Local, polite, no spammy links.
```

---

## 8. Google Business / bio blurb

```
Write a 150-word Google Business description for [BUSINESS NAME], [TRADE], serving [TOWNS].
Include phone [PHONE]. No street address. No keyword stuffing. End with a clear next step.
```

---

## Swap-in examples (North Jersey trades)

- Landscaping / hardscape — spring cleanup, patio quote, weekly maintenance  
- HVAC — no-heat, AC not cooling, tune-up  
- Roofing — leak, missing shingles, free roof check  
- General contractor — remodel consult, storm damage  

Replace [TRADE], [TOWNS], and facts with yours before generating.
