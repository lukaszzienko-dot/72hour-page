# Launch Checklist — Conversion Page Kit

Print or copy this. Check each box before you call the page “live.”

## A. Copy & details

- [ ] Business name, trade, and towns filled in (no placeholders left)
- [ ] Phone number correct and click-to-call works on mobile (`tel:+1…`)
- [ ] Email for the form is correct
- [ ] One clear offer / CTA (not five competing buttons)
- [ ] No street address on the page (city/region only is fine)
- [ ] No DOB, SSN, or other sensitive fields on the form
- [ ] No fake reviews, star counts, or “as seen in” claims

## B. Technical

- [ ] Open `page-template.html` locally — layout looks good on phone width (~390px)
- [ ] Replace favicon / title tag with your business name
- [ ] Form opens mail with subject + body (or point to your real form endpoint later)
- [ ] All links work; no `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` text visible
- [ ] Images (if you add any) are compressed and have alt text

## C. Publish (pick one free path)

- [ ] **GitHub Pages:** new public repo → upload HTML as `index.html` → enable Pages  
- [ ] **Netlify Drop / similar:** drag the file → copy the HTTPS URL  
- [ ] **Existing host:** upload via FTP/cPanel to a subdomain or `/get-quote/` path  
- [ ] Confirm the live URL loads on your phone off Wi‑Fi (cellular)

## D. Lead path test

- [ ] Tap the phone number — dialer opens with the right digits
- [ ] Submit a test lead to yourself — email arrives / mailto opens with full details
- [ ] Reply-from address is one you actually check
- [ ] Missed-call / after-hours plan: who calls back, and by when?

## E. Distribution (no ads required)

- [ ] Add the URL to Google Business Profile (website field)
- [ ] Link from Instagram / Facebook bio if you use them
- [ ] Add to email signature
- [ ] Optional: hand the URL to 5 past customers and ask for a real review on GBP

## F. After first real lead

- [ ] Respond within your promised window
- [ ] Note what the visitor asked — tighten the headline if needed
- [ ] Keep a simple log: date, town, job type, won/lost

---

**Done when:** a stranger on a phone can understand the offer in 5 seconds, call or submit, and you receive it.
